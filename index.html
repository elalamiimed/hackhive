    Rules

    House rules

    1. Two per team. Exactly two — no solos, no trios. Coming alone? The pot pairs you up.
    2. Hack Hive members only. This is an in-house event. The access code is your proof of membership.
    3. Vibecoding counts. AI-assisted or hand-written, both are fine. Ship however you work — just understand every line you submit.
    4. Desktop only. Web or native, but it runs on a desktop. Mobile is out of scope; don't burn hours on it.
    5. Repo link + video. No live demo session — the video is your demo. And write real documentation: setup, architecture, decisions, all in README.md.
    6. Frontend and architecture carry weight. Judges look hard at how it looks and how it's structured. Sloppy folders cost points.
    7. CS students come first. If it doesn't actually help a CS student survive the semester, it misses the point.
    8. Judges' decisions are final. Criteria: does it work, is it usable, is it thoughtful. No appeals, no hard feelings.

    Grab a friend, or don't — the pot will find you one. Either way, build something you'd actually use next semester.
